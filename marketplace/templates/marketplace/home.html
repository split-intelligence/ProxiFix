About us

Built on one belief: finding a good tradesperson shouldn't be this hard.

HandiGO gives customers a clearer way to explain the problem, browse real worker profiles nearby, and stay in the loop once work begins. For workers, it's a platform where consistency pays — where showing up, doing good work, and building a track record actually means something.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

ProxiFix (HandiGO) is committed to protecting your privacy. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

  • Account information you provide (name, email, password).
  • Profile details such as skills, location, and public reputational data.
  • Job and application data necessary to operate the marketplace.
  • Usage and analytics data to improve the service.

How we use information

We use data to operate and improve the platform, process payments, communicate with you, and provide relevant search and matching features. We may also use anonymized analytics to understand platform usage.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may share data with service providers who help operate the platform (payment processors, hosting, email providers) and when required by law.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no system is completely secure. If you have concerns, contact us at the site support address.

Your choices

You can edit your profile and communication preferences in your account. To delete your account or request exported data, contact support.

Changes

We may update this policy; we will post changes here with the updated date.

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us through the usual support channels.

About HandiGO

Built on one belief: getting the right worker for the job shouldn't be this hard.

HandiGO is the local repair marketplace for customers who need skilled help nearby — and for tradespeople who want a platform where their work history actually counts. From plumbing and electrical to engine faults and appliance repairs, the jobs that used to live in scattered chats and word-of-mouth referrals now have a better home.

You can describe the problem clearly, browse real worker profiles, check ratings and completed jobs, and stay in the loop from first quote to final fix — without chasing anyone down.

More than just home repairs

From vehicles and engines to appliances and general maintenance — HandiGO covers the jobs that matter, not just the ones that fit a narrow category.

Know who you're hiring before you commit

Browse worker profiles, completed job counts, ratings, response times, and level badges — so choosing feels like a decision, not a gamble.
